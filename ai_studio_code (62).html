About Our Masjid

A Pillar of Devotion, Community & Shafi'i Tradition

"Our masjid is a sanctuary for the remembrance of Allah (SWT), serving the spiritual, educational, and welfare needs of Muslims in Cape Town."

Our Story & Heritage

Established to serve the Cape Town Muslim community, Masjid Ighwaanul Muslimeen continues to foster authentic Islamic scholarship, unity, and brotherhood.

Our Sacred Mission

To provide daily congregational prayers, exemplary youth education, welfare relief, and authentic guidance according to the Qur'an and Sunnah.

Our Community Values

Sincerity, compassionate support for the sick and bereaved, youth empowerment, and transparent stewardship of community trusts.
