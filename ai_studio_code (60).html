About Our Masjid

A Place of Worship, Community & Sacred Learning

"Our masjid is a sanctuary for the remembrance of Allah (SWT), established to nurture faith, provide authentic Islamic guidance, and serve the social and spiritual welfare of our community."

Our Story & Legacy

Founded to serve the growing Muslim population in the Western Cape, Masjid Ighwaanul Muslimeen continues to be a pillar of unity, adhering to the Qur'an and Sunnah with traditional reverence.

Our Sacred Mission

To cultivate a vibrant, compassionate community anchored in Islamic morals, educational excellence, youth mentorship, and active charitable outreach.

Our Community Values

Sincerity (Ikhlas), unity across all backgrounds, dedicated care for elders, empowerment of our youth, and maintaining transparent management of community trusts.
